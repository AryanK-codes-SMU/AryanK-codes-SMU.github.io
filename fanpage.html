01 / ABOUT

MORE THAN AN ACTOR

A career built on reinvention.

Born on 10 January 1974 in Mumbai, Hrithik Roshan made his lead-actor debut in 2000 with Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai. The film became a major success and turned him into an instant star.

Across the years he has moved between romance, drama, action, science fiction, historical cinema and biographical stories — often disappearing into very different characters.
